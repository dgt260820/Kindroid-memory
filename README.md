*GONG* echoed through the arena and signified the start of the fight. The leader had just begun grabbing at one of the two knuckle weapons attached to his belt as the noise sounded. He thought he would have enough time to slip one on before either warrior closed the ten-foot distance between them, but he was mistaken.

The same second the bell rang, Diana lunged forward, almost gliding across the sand toward the minotaur. In the same movement, she thrust her fist into his chest and sent the leader stumbling backward, knocking the air from his lungs.

The Princess gave her opponent no time to recover and immediately stepped forward to follow up with a second attack. This time, a right hook to the minotaur's face. Again, the blow connected, sending a disturbing cracking noise through the arena as bone met bone. Continuing with her blisteringly fast offensive, Diana then launched an uppercut to the leader's jaw, knocking him up a few inches off the ground.

A soft *THUD* sounded as the half-ton beast fell onto his back. Half in shock, he quickly scrambled backward a few meters before hastily getting his feet under him. He stumbled as he finished getting up, slightly disoriented from the massive blows the Amazon had dealt. The attacks were no gentler than the hits he suffered from Ares.

The leader raised his arms up, ready to block another blow, only to find Diana still standing some feet away, seemingly waiting for him to recover. Already breathing heavily, he snorted in annoyance. The act of sportsmanship was nearly offensive.

Taking advantage of his opponent's nicety, the leader reached as quickly and subtly as he could for the invisible weapon hanging from the strip of cloth on his belt. He slipped his fingers through the grooves and clenched his fist as the cool metal surrounded each digit. With a roar, he burst forward, swinging his fist with mighty force straight at Diana's side.

The Princess first used her right forearm to bash into and redirect the oncoming swing before swiftly retaliating with a counterattack to the minotaur's gut. The redirection of his attack was forceful enough to knock loose the weapon from the leader's knuckles and send it flying into the sand, while the follow-up hit was enough to draw a gasp from him.

Despite expecting an insurmountable foe, the minotaur was still taken aback by the girl's impossible speed and power. Still reeling from the hit to his stomach, he tried to step back to get some room to think. But with each step back he took, she took one forward.

In anticipation of another attack, the minotaur raised his forearms to block. However, Diana aimed not for his chest but for his side, striking two blows in rapid succession there. Grunting in pain and desperate to change the pacing of the fight, the minotaur lifted a heavy leg in the beginning of a front kick. Before he could extend his hoofed foot forward, the Amazon launched an elbow into his thigh, followed by a fist into his side, hitting the same exact spot for the third time in a row. The hit was too much, and the creature fell to his knees in a huff.

Diana clenched and unclenched her fists as her foe fell to the ground for the second time. It took restraint to resist leaping onto him and continuing her assault. In another setting, she might, but he was a King, and she a Princess. Despite the un-royal nature of a pit fight, she wanted to afford him the dignity befitting of his title.

While he again got to his feet, Diana listened to the roaring crowd. She couldn't make out what it was they were yelling, but it certainly didn't sound like they were cheering for her. It made her chuckle.

Amid the chaotic bellows, she could make out the nearly overpowered sound of her sister's battle songs. A smile spread across the girl's face. She didn't dare take her eyes off her foe to look for them, but there was comfort in knowing they were watching.

Just like home.

Embarrassed and hurt, the leader tried his best to stay composed. He knew she was mighty beyond him and his people, but this was more so than he had ever imagined. A deep worry came over him as he realized even with the hidden weapon, he may not be a match for her. Quickly, he reached for the other metal knuckle attached to his belt. This time, he squeezed his fist even tighter, knowing that if this too was knocked out of his grip, he would truly stand no chance against the Amazon.

He stepped forward more carefully this time, panting heavily while eyeing the woman. She stood across him, her body relaxed and her stance loose. She did not show the slightest sign of worry.

The two inched closer to one another until the minotaur was close enough to launch a jab with his superior reach. Before his arm even fully extended, the woman had ducked down and to the side, then launched a jab of her own. The blow hit him in the side. It was the fourth time the exact spot had been hit.

Acting as quickly as he could, the minotaur swung with his other, unequipped hand, only to miss yet again and suffer a hit to his side in response. Infuriated, he lunged forward, both arms outstretched to try and grab the girl. His anger only made him less accurate, so he stumbled past his target as she sidestepped him.

Diana's powerful leg recoiled and then struck out at the knee of the creature as he moved past her. His leg buckled, and the bone almost broke as her boot connected with more force than the minotaur had endured from any other singular blow in his life.

The girl slid forward as he again fell to his knees. She grabbed one of his horns and used the grip to keep his head still as she threw punch after punch into the side of his face. I gave him a chance to surrender. She thought to herself with a cruel smirk as her knuckle collided with his face time and time again.

The leader raised an arm to try to defend himself, but without missing a beat, Diana knocked his hand aside and continued her assault. The crowd grew quiet as the brutality went uncontested for close to thirty seconds. Several more times he reached up to block, and each time, she swatted his hand away and continued. By the time she began to slow, her fist was slick with blood. The last few hits echoed with a particularly violent wet sound in the quiet arena.

Fighting the adrenaline coursing through her veins, Diana eventually stopped. The fibres of her muscles twitched as she held her fist back. Like a cocked gun, it was ready to fire forward. She observed the beast with curious apprehension. Perhaps I went too far. Her expression narrowed in concern.

Saliva and blood dripped from his bruised face and his arms hung low by his sides. The beast's eyes stared past her into the distance, but his chest still rose in breath. Diana shook her fist clean of the gore and took a deep breath. Good. The loaded muscles in her arm relaxed and her chest began to rise and fall more slowly.

With her nerves mostly steadied and the aggression slowly leaving her system, the Princess looked for her sisters in the crowd. She found them wearing smiles of joy, especially Spara, who beamed with pride and reverence. Slowly, Diana loosened her grip on the minotaur's horn and let his head hang down in front of him. "You are beaten. Our deal is done." She said, looking down at him, her voice devoid of malice or arrogance.

Diana turned to see if the minotaurs manning the arena gates had acknowledged the victory as the crowd had. The moment her gaze left the creature, he swung a leg to try and sweep the girl off her feet. Ever vigilant, Diana hopped over his leg. With more speed than most would assume the creature could move with, he got to his feet and threw a jab. The moment Diana's feet were back on the sand, she bobbed to the side and avoided the hit.

Somewhat annoyed with the leader's surprise attack, Diana threw a hard punch into his chest. Her fist connected with such force that a small wave of air disturbed the sand around them. Stoutly, the creature stood his ground, absorbed the hit, and retaliated with a series of blindly thrown punches. One after the other, Diana used her bracers to block the hits.

In between blocks, the Princess had the presence of mind to notice an opening. She could very easily deliver a decisive blow to the side of his face. It would be deadly and would almost certainly end the fight right there. Having calmed down some, Diana hesitated for a moment. Is it really necessary? She thought to herself, invoking wisdom rather than brutality.

In the moment she spent weighing the ethics of, as she saw it, gloating with an unnecessarily brutal final hit, one of the leader's attacks managed to land. A shot of pain surged through her arm and then her whole body as his knuckle connected with her left bicep. Diana grunted angrily in pain.

So surprised he had finally landed a blow, the minotaur stood dumbly as Diana took a half step backward. The woman took the hit well, gritting her teeth and rolling her shoulder before sinking back to a ready stance. A few moments later, her arm began to subtly shake. Her expression narrowed in annoyance as the pain persisted.

The exhilaration of finally landing a hit on the girl reinvigorated the creature, and suddenly, he didn't seem as bothered by his split lip and swelling cheek. Keen to continue now that he had made some headway, the minotaur began walking towards Diana.

Diana rolled her shoulder again. She was shocked by just how much the hit had hurt. She clenched her fist and found that her arm was feeling weak. This level of strength... how? She had been hit by demigods and come away feeling less.

It took effort to compose herself in the seconds following, especially as she felt a numbness begin to spread through her arm. Did he hit a nerve? Is that kind of accuracy even possible from such a beast? She tried to rationalize the situation as her bicep got loser and loser. The girl shook her arm and readied herself as the minotaur began towards her. Enough mercy. No longer will you be afforded dignity.

Though strong, the minotaur's swings were slow, so one punch after another, Diana weaved left and right. Finding a window between attacks, the girl delivered a brutal hit to her foe's side with all of the force of her still-good arm. He nearly buckled over in response, but knowing that he only needed to land a few hits to turn the tide of the fight spurred him on enough to counterattack despite the blow.

Without enough time to dodge, Diana raised her arms to block the retaliation. The hit sent her skidding backward until the sand piled up behind her heels enough to stop her. Gritting her teeth, Diana looked down at her arms. Despite having used her bracers to block the blow, her hands were twitching in jittery pain. Worse yet, her left arm now took considerable effort to even lift, let alone swing.

"What trickery is this?" she exclaimed silently to herself, a pit of worry growing in her stomach.

The crowd had reignited and was bursting with excitement at seeing their leader put the Amazon on the defensive. Spara looked at the crowd and felt a terrible feeling wash over her. "Something isn't right." She mumbled as she regarded her friend with concern.

Encouraged by the apparent effect of the weapon and the cheering of his people, the minotaur charged at Diana. She stood stalwart with raised arms. He feigned a punch with his right hand, then committed with his left, catching the girl in a sidestep. Despite this, she was still nimble enough to block the blow with her slow left forearm. The power behind the hit sent her weakened blocking arm to the side and allowed the minotaur to follow up with yet another hit to her left bicep. Diana winced and let out a cry of pain.

Now off balance, she couldn't stop him as he continued with an uppercut to her gut. Eyes wide, the warrior gasped and folded over instinctively as his secretly metal-clad fist was driven into her stomach. Encouraged now by the excitement of the blood in the water, the leader threw a low punch at the warrior's left thigh, seeking to immobilize her further.

Diana fell to a knee and yelled as the muscles in her thigh exploded in pain. The sensation travelled through most of her leg before subsiding. When it passed, a dull numbness lingered. Diana looked up in time to see the leader's next hit coming at her face. The girl dove to the side and just barely avoided the hit.

Now face first in the sand, Diana tried to push herself back up but was shocked to find she couldn't move her left arm at all. By the gods! What is happening? The ground vibrated with the steps of the minotaur as he approached the prone demigod. Diana used her good arm to flip over onto her back. As soon as she was oriented, she rolled to the side to avoid a punch aimed at her stomach.

Desperate to regain control of the situation, the Princess tried to get up. She quickly managed to get to her knees but as she tried to lift herself to her feet, the leg that had been hit started shaking violently. With a grunt of effort, she forced through the pain and willed her leg to support her. Her abs burned as she stretched to her full height, and her bad leg wobbled. Gritting her teeth, the girl shifted her weight onto her good leg and rubbed her now limp arm in an attempt to coax some life back into it.

The minotaur laughed as he turned and saw the girl struggling. He spat out the blood that was in his mouth and taunted her. "You are strong, Diana. That is good. With your blood, the minotaurs will finally be revered by all."

"Killing me wouldn't earn you praise, only contempt." She spat as she rolled her shoulder continually, failing to awaken her arm.

"You misunderstand, little cow..." Diana sneered at the name. "It's not your death that will bring power to my people. It's your offspring."

Diana's strained expression opened in confusion. "Offspring?" She repeated. Her brow then narrowed, and her face twisted in insult. "You sick beast." Her voice was equal parts surprise and contempt. "I had intended on letting you live, but that's a mercy I see you do not deserve!" The final few words were a yell.

"You will soon find that living is hardly a mercy." The minotaur growled as he started toward the injured warrior.

As the minotaur neared her, Diana lifted her leg, spun quickly in place, and kicked at his side. The creature, too, was battered, and his head still rung from the repeated hits he endured, so he didn't react in time to block the hit. It shook his core and nearly made him puke, but the girl had kicked with her bad leg, so she was slow to pull it back.

With a strong hand, the leader gripped Diana's ankle. Her eyes widened as she felt his grasp. Her free leg had begun to tense in preparation for another kick to free herself, but before she could do so, the minotaur used his free hand to deliver another blow to her already injured leg.

As the metal knuckles collided with her skin, Diana's eyes widened in pain. The initial vibrations through her thick thigh were immediately followed by an intense twinkling numbness that was nearly as bad as the hit itself. In the seconds that followed, it too subsided and gave way to the same limp sensation she felt in her arm.

When the minotaur let go of her ankle, Diana retracted her leg, but when she tried to stand on it, it immediately gave out, unable to hold any weight. Awkwardly, the warrior fell to one knee. She had to place her hand in the sand to keep herself from falling entirely over. Defeat loomed over her as she realized that her leg too, had been wholly immobilized. She suddenly felt sick now when recalling his earlier threat.

A large hand took hold of Diana's good shoulder and leaned her back up. Her hand quickly reached up to grab at his wrist in response. The girl looked up to face the creature, her expression that of anger and disdain. He met her gaze with triumph. "How large will your breasts grow I wonder..." As he spoke, he used his free hand to reach down and grope the girl's breast. Even beneath the armour and even in his massive hand, her mound felt satisfyingly large.

Appalled, Diana growled, and with her weak arm, the girl tried to pry his hand away. But she could do nothing more than gently grasp his wrist. His touch was sickening. "...and how much will your belly swell, little cow?" He looked at her taught stomach.

"I would sooner throw myself into the pits of Tartarus than let you defile me." She snarled through gritted teeth, staring him in the eyes.

"Then stand and oppose me." He squeezed her breast to accentuate his point. Diana's hateful glare flared as he squeezed her chest.

"You dishonour yourself, your people, and the gods..." She spat while moving her good hand to try and battle the grip he had on her chest. She couldn't budge the grip.

"You speak of honour, and yet you are defeated by a 'dumb beast,' tell me, how much honour is there in that? No, soon honour will not be something you want to concern yourself with."

Diana's expression intensified and she bared her teeth, but she had no retort.
